About the AGM

The 11th FEWACCI Annual General Meeting brings together West Africa’s organized private sector, regional institutions, policymakers and international partners to strengthen private-sector participation in the regional integration process.

The meeting takes place at a time when West Africa continues to advance regional economic integration through free movement, trade liberalisation and common sectoral policies, while businesses still face structural challenges including infrastructure gaps, regulatory fragmentation and limited trade facilitation systems.

The AGM will provide a platform to promote structured Public–Private Dialogue, accelerate digital transformation in trade and investment, strengthen cross-border business partnerships and adopt key institutional and strategic decisions for FEWACCI.

Participants

Held under the High Patronage of Her Excellency Mrs. Nialé KABA, Minister of State, Minister of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation of the Republic of Côte d'Ivoire,and under the Chairmanship of the Minister of Commerce, Industry and Crafts, the Federation of West African Chambers of Commerce and Industry (FEWACCI) will hold its 11th General Assembly on 28 and 29 September 2026 in Abidjan, at the headquarters of the Chamber of Commerce and Industry of Côte d'Ivoire. This important gathering will bring together the leaders of the region's Chambers of Commerce and Industry, as well as institutional and private sector representatives, to address issues related to strengthening regional economic integration, developing trade and investment, and consolidating public-private dialogue in West Africa.
