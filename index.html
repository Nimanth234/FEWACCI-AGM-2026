Welcome to the 11th FEWACCI Annual General Meeting

The Federation of West African Chambers of Commerce and Industry (FEWACCI) convenes its 11th Annual General Meeting in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire.

The meeting will bring together leaders of Chambers of Commerce and Industry, regional institutions, policymakers, development partners and private-sector representatives to strengthen regional economic integration, trade, investment and private-sector cooperation across West Africa.

AGM Highlights

Regional Dialogue

High-level dialogue between the West African private sector, regional institutions and policymakers.

General Assembly

FEWACCI statutory discussions, institutional priorities and election of the new Bureau.

Regional Integration

Advancing practical private-sector engagement in trade, investment and regional economic integration.
